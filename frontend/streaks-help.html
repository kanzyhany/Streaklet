🔥 How Streaks & Rewards Work

Build consistency, earn points, and protect your progress with grace days!

📊 What Are Streaks?

A streak is the number of consecutive days you've completed a specific habit. Each habit tracks its own streak independently.

Example: If you complete "Drink Water" every day for 7 days in a row, you have a 7-day streak for that habit. If you miss a day, your streak resets to 0.

How Streaks Build:

  • Mark a habit as "Done" today → Streak increases by 1
  • Complete it again tomorrow → Streak increases to 2
  • Keep the chain going! → Streak keeps growing
  • Miss a day or mark as "Missed" → Streak resets to 0

⭐ Points System

Earn points automatically when you reach streak milestones! Points work differently for personal vs. shared calendars.

Personal Calendar Example:
  1. Complete habit for 5 days → Get 5 points (5/10)
  2. Continue to 10 days → Get 5 more points (10/10) → 1 Grace Day Awarded!

Shared Calendar Example:

  4. Member C reaches 5-day streak → Calendar gets +2 points (8/10)
  5. Member B reaches 10-day streak → Calendar gets +2 points (10/10) → Everyone gets 1 grace day!

🛡️ Grace Days

Grace days are your safety net! Use them to protect your streaks when life gets in the way.

How Grace Days Work:

  • Click "Use Grace Day" button before or during a day you might miss
  • If you skip a habit that day, your streak won't reset
  • Grace days are consumed when used, even if you don't skip
  • Personal grace days only protect personal calendar habits
  • Shared grace days only protect shared calendar habits
Grace Day Example: You have a 12-day streak on "Morning Exercise" but you're traveling tomorrow:
  1. Today: Click "Use Grace Day" (Grace days: 1 → 0)
  2. Tomorrow: Even if you don't exercise, your streak stays at 12!
  3. Day after: Resume exercising normally, streak continues

Important Notes:

  • Grace days can only be activated once per day
  • If you try to activate twice, you'll see: "Grace day for today is already activated"
  • Grace days protect ALL your habits for that calendar on that day
  • Use them wisely - they take time to earn!
